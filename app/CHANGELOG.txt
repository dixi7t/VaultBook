VaultBook (formerly OfflineBooks US) — Changelog
================================================

1.55.2  (2026-11-20)  No black band at the bottom on iPhone
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * When VaultBook was added to the Home Screen, a black band could cover
    the bottom of the screen and the tab bar sat too high. The app no
    longer draws under the status bar (status bar style "default"), so iOS
    gives it the full height; the status bar takes the page colour in light
    and dark. Remove the Home Screen icon and add it again to pick this up.

1.55.1  (2026-11-20)  Version numbers
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Every change now ships as its own version: a git tag (v1.55.1) and a
    GitHub Release with these notes, so any version can be found and
    restored. The version shows in Settings > About and in backups.

1.55.0  (2026-11-20)  Faster start
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Faster start: the published page loads one script and one stylesheet
    instead of 99 files (built by app/tools/build-site.py from the new
    source page app/index.src.html). Their names carry a content hash, so
    the offline copy serves them instantly on later visits and a phone only
    downloads what changed after an update.

1.54.1  (2026-11-20)  Contrast and offline-cache fixes
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Accessibility audit (Impeccable method, light and dark): every text and
    control on iPhone and iPad screens now meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1).
    Links use Apple's Action Blue #0066CC (light) and #409CFF (dark); filled
    buttons use #0066CC / #0071E3 so white text passes; money in is
    #1E7B34; badges #D70015; tab labels and the selected tab are readable.
    Today's date in the calendar, the overdue legend in dark mode and the
    logo preview label were fixed too. Activity month headings are now h2.
  * The offline cache now always checks for a newer file, so an update
    shows at once instead of after the browser's own cache expires.

1.54.0  (2026-11-20)  Passcode screen
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Passcode screen before VaultBook opens on the hosted site (iPhone-style
    keypad; keyboard digits, Backspace and Enter also work). It stays open
    until the tab or app is closed. Five wrong tries pause entry for a
    minute. It keeps casual visitors out; it is not encryption. To protect
    your books, turn on the company password in Settings.

1.53.1  (2026-11-20)  Touch fixes on every screen
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Touch audit of every screen (107 on iPhone, 97 on iPad portrait and
    landscape): no screen scrolls sideways, no text is under 11pt, and no
    control is under 28pt. Buttons, fields and menus are at least 36pt on
    touch screens (the form F-key strip, paste grid, calendar, reclassify,
    report sorting, command bar); text links in reports get a taller tap
    area; the iPad menu bar fits the width and its last menus open
    leftward; report date and basis sit above the title on a phone; the
    calendar month grid on a phone leaves the daily balance to the Week
    and Agenda views.

1.53.0  (2026-11-20)  Bank feed on iPhone and iPad; iOS look
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Bank feed on iPhone and iPad (Bank Feed tab, Home, and the + button):
    add a bank or credit card account, then type statement lines one after
    another (date steps a day at a time, the description remembers earlier
    ones) or paste many at once from a spreadsheet or Notes, for a month or
    a whole year. Unsaved lines are kept on the device. Saved lines wait in
    For review, where each shows the category your rules or past choices
    suggest, offers to match an entry already in VaultBook, and posts with
    one tap; Accept all posts every suggested line. Card payments and
    transfers are chosen as the other account. Lines already entered with
    the same date, description and amount are skipped.
  * iPhone and iPad use iOS system colors, the system font, grouped lists,
    a floating tab bar with a separate + button, and capsule buttons, in
    light and dark appearance.

1.47.0  (2026-11-18)  Update QuickBooks to match VaultBook
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * New: QuickBooks Desktop > Update QuickBooks. VaultBook compares the
    transactions it took from QuickBooks with a fresh QuickBooks export
    and sends only what you changed in VaultBook: reclassified lines,
    amounts, memos, dates, check numbers, payees, and voids. Accounts
    QuickBooks does not have are created first. Nothing is deleted;
    entries that exist only in QuickBooks are never touched.
  * Every edit carries QuickBooks' edit stamp: an entry changed in

    overwritten. Voids are asked separately.
  * The connector (VaultBook QuickBooks.cmd) gains 7 Connect to
    VaultBook: VaultBook talks to its window directly on this computer,
    using the link code it shows, to get a fresh export and send the
    changes; you still answer every Y/N in the tool. 8 Install the link
    (one time, no admin rights) lets "Open the QuickBooks tool" in
    VaultBook start it.
  * Without the link: Download the update file for 2 Send to QuickBooks,
    and Load a QuickBooks export from 5 Export everything.

1.46.0  (2026-11-18)  Close the books: VaultBook prepares, you review and sign off
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * New page Close the books (side menu, Gateway > Banking, command
    palette). VaultBook does the staff accountant's part; you review,
    ask the client, tie out and sign off. The checklist:
    1 Statements received - each bank and card account, month by month.
    2 Prepared by VaultBook - a self-test on last year, then Prepare.
    3 Review - by payee, largest dollars first, accept a whole group.
    4 Client questions - kept on the bank lines, copy or download them,
      type the answers back.
    5 Bank reconciliations - each account through the last month ended.
    6 Compare to last year - changes over a floor set per client need
      a reason.
    7 Adjusting entries - last year's journal entries for the same
      period, one click to copy.
    8 Sign off - only when 1 to 6 tie; locks the period (closing date)
      and records it in the audit trail.
  * Prepare works in this order: an entry already in the books is
    matched (never entered twice), then your bank rules, then each
    payee's habit from the last two years. Only sure habits are posted,
    each with its reason and undo in the bank feed. Owner, loan,
    payroll, tax and balance sheet accounts always wait for you.
  * Self-test: VaultBook learns from the years before last year,
    predicts last year and shows how often it was right. Auto-posting is
    offered when at least 20 lines were sure and 95% of them right.
  * Mixed habits show up to three accounts with how often each was used.

1.45.1  (2026-11-18)  Common merchants: 1,487 names
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * The Common merchants list grows from 326 to 1,487 US merchants
    (fuel, vehicle, meals, travel, utilities, phone and internet,

    fees). Names are kept in their own file, js/merchants.js.

  * Statement PDFs: a name and its amount printed a point apart are now

1.3.0  (2026-10-15)  Company password protection
-----------------------------------------------
  + Optional password per company (Settings -> Security). Protection is real
    encryption at rest, not just a login screen:
      - a random 256-bit data key encrypts every stored record with AES-GCM;
        each record has its own IV and is bound to its store and id, so records
        can't be altered or swapped without detection;
      - the data key is wrapped with a key derived from the password
        (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 600,000 rounds) and separately with a one-time
        recovery key; no password or password hash is stored;
      - uses the browser's built-in WebCrypto (verified available on file://
        in Chromium); no libraries.
  + Unlock screen with password hint, recovery-key unlock (forces a new
    password and issues a new recovery key), and growing delays after wrong
    passwords.
  + Lock company button, auto-lock after 5/15/30/60 minutes of inactivity.
    Locking clears the books, dialogs and messages from the screen and memory.
  + Change password, issue new recovery key, turn protection off (all require
    the password; all recorded in the audit log).
  + Backups of protected companies are encrypted; restoring one asks for the
    password or recovery key and keeps the restored company protected.
  + Company list marks protected companies.
  + Storage: company databases upgrade from layout v1 to v2 (adds an
    unencrypted "meta" store holding only the wrapped keys). Companies from
    1.0-1.2 upgrade automatically on first open; tested.
  + Fixed while testing: after locking and unlocking, type-to-search fields
    stopped showing suggestions (a detached list was being reused).
  Measured (20,000 transactions, Chromium/Linux): turning protection on 2.6 s
  once; unlock after browser restart 1.45 s (1.1 s unprotected); saving a
  transaction 10 ms.
  Tests: engine 30/30, in-browser self-test 35/35 (adds raw-storage-is-
  encrypted and 1.2 database upgrade checks), end-to-end 105/105 (adds the
  full security flow including browser restart and auto-lock).

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
-----------------
Storage
  - Books live in this browser profile's IndexedDB, not in the app folder.
    Clearing site data, InPrivate/Incognito windows, a different browser, or a
    different Windows user means the data is not visible. Regular backups are
    essential. Browsers can evict storage under severe disk pressure unless
    persistent storage is granted (Settings -> Storage and about).
  - In Chrome/Edge all pages opened from file:// share one storage origin.
    Other local HTML files you open could, in principle, read this data.
  - Only one browser tab should edit a company at a time. A second tab does not
    see changes made in the first until reloaded.

Features not included
  - Inventory/items with quantity on hand, payroll, sales tax calculation and
    filing, 1099 e-filing, locations as a separate dimension, multi-currency,
    online bank feeds, emailing invoices, attachments, users/permissions.

Password protection
  - Forgotten password + lost recovery key = data cannot be recovered.
  - Company names remain visible in the company list.
  - Protects data at rest only: not while the company is unlocked, and not
    against malware on the PC. One password per company; no user roles.
  - The in-app delay after wrong passwords only slows guessing in this app;
    against someone who copies the files, the protection is the password's
    strength plus 600,000 PBKDF2 rounds. Use a long password.
  - Auto-lock discards unsaved form entries.

Company Snapshot
  - Snapshot layouts are display preferences stored in this browser (not in
    the company data or backups), so they don't move to another PC with a
    backup restore.
  - Panels are not resizable in height or width beyond the column choice;
    use Expand for the full view.
  - The Customer tab summarizes customers only (no vendor snapshot).

Layout and tables
  - In a long General Ledger the column headers stay visible, but the account
    name above each section scrolls away.
  - Column layouts are matched by header text; reports whose columns change
    (e.g. P&L by month) keep a separate layout per column mode.
  - Preferences live in this browser's localStorage, which Chrome/Edge share
    between all locally opened HTML files.
  - On phones, tables scroll sideways but headers are not sticky.

Design and accessibility
  - Verified with automated tools (axe-core) and keyboard-driven tests, not
    yet with a full manual screen-reader session (Narrator/NVDA) or by users
    with disabilities.
  - The phone layout is usable but the app is designed for Windows desktops;
    there is no installable mobile app.
  - Spacing values are rem-based and consistent in practice but not all
    expressed as tokens yet.

Behaviour to be aware of
  - PDF output uses the browser's print dialog ("Save as PDF"); there is no
    built-in PDF generator.
  - Cash basis: payments are allocated proportionally across the lines of the
    invoice/bill they pay. Unapplied payments and journal entries to A/R or A/P
    stay on the balance sheet. Payments applied to credit memos/vendor credits
    are not re-allocated.
  - Reports show up to 5,000-6,000 lines on screen; exports include all lines.
  - Reconciliation ticks are saved as "cleared" immediately; there is no separate
    draft. Voiding or editing a reconciled transaction requires undoing the
    reconciliation first (only the most recent reconciliation can be undone).
  - The data integrity check verifies the books; it cannot repair them. If it
    reports problems, restore a backup.
  - Visual design uses fonts included with Windows 11 (Segoe UI, Bahnschrift).
    During development it was checked with substitute fonts on Linux; the exact
    appearance on Windows was not screenshot-verified.
  - Automated testing used Chromium (the engine behind Chrome and Edge) over
    file://. The app was not run on a physical Windows 11 machine during
    development.
