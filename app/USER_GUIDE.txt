1. FIRST-TIME SETUP
-------------------
a) Create the company. Choose:
   - Accounting basis. Accrual (income when invoiced, expenses when billed) or
     Cash (income when money arrives, expenses when paid). You can switch any
     report between the two at any time; this only sets the default.
   - First month of the fiscal year (January for most small businesses).
b) Review the chart of accounts (Chart of accounts). A standard US list is
   created for you. Add, rename, renumber or make sub-accounts as you like.
c) Enter opening balances. Pick a start date (usually the last day before your
   fiscal year). For each bank, credit card, loan and asset account, click
   "Opening balance" in the Chart of accounts and enter the statement balance.
   These post against "Opening Balance Equity".
   - Customers who owed you money on the start date: enter an invoice dated on
     or before the start date for each unpaid amount.
   - Bills you still owed: enter each as a bill.
   This keeps customer and vendor balances correct.
d) Add customers and vendors (Customers / Vendors, or "+ New" inside any form).
e) Settings and backup -> Company: enter your address (printed on invoices).
f) Download your first backup.


2. HOW THE BOOKS WORK
---------------------
Every transaction is recorded as debits and credits that always balance.
OfflineBooks creates these for you; you can see them under "Journal entry
(debits and credits)" at the bottom of any saved transaction.

  Sales receipt $1,000      Debit Checking 1,000   / Credit Sales 1,000
  Invoice $1,000            Debit A/R 1,000        / Credit Sales 1,000
  Payment $400 on invoice   Debit Checking 400     / Credit A/R 400
  Bill $300                 Debit Expense 300      / Credit A/P 300
  Bill payment $100         Debit A/P 100          / Credit Checking 100
  Transfer $500             Debit Savings 500      / Credit Checking 500

Payments never count as income a second time and bill payments never count as
expenses a second time: they only move money against A/R or A/P. Transfers
never create income or expenses.


3. EVERYDAY TASKS
-----------------
Click "+ New" (or press Alt+N) for the full list.

CHOOSING ACCOUNTS, CUSTOMERS AND VENDORS (type to search)
Account fields and customer/vendor fields work like search boxes:
- Type part of a name or an account number ("util", "6600", "office sup").
  Suggestions appear as you type; ↓/↑ to move, Enter or Tab to choose.
- If what you need doesn't exist, the last suggestion is "+ Add new account"
  (or "+ Add new customer/vendor"). Choose it and a small window opens with the
  name already filled in. Pick the type, save, and you're returned to the same
  line of your transaction with the new account selected and the cursor in
  the next field. Nothing you already typed is lost.
- Click the field (or press ↓) to see the full list.
- If you leave a field with text that matches nothing, it turns red.

FAST ENTRY
- Enter moves to the next field; Ctrl+S saves the transaction.
- A blank line is added automatically when you fill the last line.
- Journal entries: when you choose the account on a new line, the amount
  needed to balance the entry is filled in for you on the debit or credit side.
- Date fields: t = today, + = next day, - = previous day.
- Amounts are tidied to two decimals when you leave the field.

CUSTOMERS
- Estimate: a quote for a customer. It does not affect your books. Open it and
  choose Create Invoice to bill it; the estimate is then closed. (If you void
  that invoice, the estimate opens again.) Mark closed / Reopen by hand.
- Purchase Order: an order to a vendor; also does not affect your books.
  Create bill turns it into a bill and closes it.
- Invoice: customer owes you. The screen follows QuickBooks Desktop's Create
  Invoices window. Pick the CUSTOMER:JOB (the BILL TO box and TERMS fill in),
  then enter lines: ITEM (a product/service, or the income account),
  DESCRIPTION, QTY and RATE (AMOUNT = QTY x RATE, or type the amount). TERMS
  set the due date ("Due on receipt" = the invoice date).
  CUSTOMER MESSAGE prints at the bottom of the invoice; "< Add New >" adds
  your own message to the list. MEMO is for you.
  Save & Close (Ctrl+S), Save & New (Ctrl+E), Revert (throw away changes).
  The arrows next to Find step to the previous / next invoice.
- Receive payment: choose the customer; open invoices are listed. Enter the
  amount and click "Apply oldest first", or type amounts per invoice. Partial
  payments are fine; the remainder stays open. Money left over is kept as a
  credit on the customer's account.
  You cannot apply more than an invoice's open balance.
- Sales receipt: a sale paid immediately (no invoice).
- Credit memo: reduces what a customer owes (return, discount). Apply it to an
  invoice, or leave it as a credit.
- Customer refund: pays a customer credit back (an overpayment or credit memo).
- Statement: open a customer -> Statement -> Print / Save as PDF.

VENDORS
- Bill: you owe a vendor. Pay bills: choose the vendor and apply the payment.
- Expense: money spent directly from a bank or credit card account.
- Check: an expense paid by paper check (check number auto-increments).
- Vendor credit: a vendor's credit that reduces what you owe; apply to a bill.
- Credit card credit: a refund on a card.

OTHER
- Deposit: money into a bank account that isn't a customer payment (owner
  investment, loan proceeds, refunds). Choose the "received from" account.
- Transfer: between your own accounts, including paying a credit card bill from
  checking (Transfer from Checking to Business Credit Card).
- Journal entry: advanced; any accounts, debits must equal credits. Lines that
  use A/R or A/P need a customer or vendor.
  Don't use a journal entry to record a customer paying an invoice (or you
  paying a bill): the invoice/bill would stay open. Use Receive payment / Pay
  bills. The journal form shows a reminder when it sees this pattern.

Printing invoices: open the invoice -> Print. Download PDF saves a PDF file;
Print sends it to a printer. To put your logo on invoices: Settings and
backup -> Company -> Invoice logo (PNG or JPEG). There is no logo by default.

MAKE DEPOSITS
Record customer payments and sales receipts to Undeposited Funds, then
Banking -> Make deposits: tick the ones in today's bank deposit and save.
The deposit moves them to the bank account as one amount, like your bank
statement shows.

PRINTING CHECKS
Tick "Print later" on a check, bill payment or expense (from a bank
account). Banking -> Print checks: tick the checks, enter the first check
number on your check stock, and print (US Letter voucher checks, at 100%).
Print a test on plain paper first and use Move right / Move down to line it
up. Then choose "Checks printed correctly" to record the numbers.

WRITE-OFFS AND FINANCE CHARGES
An invoice you will never collect: open it -> More -> Write off as bad debt.
Sales -> Assess finance charges charges interest on overdue balances (annual
rate x days overdue / 365, with a minimum charge and grace period).

1099 FORMS
On the 1099 page tick W-9 for vendors who gave you one. Section 4 shows the
1096 totals, any problems to fix, Recipient copies (PDF, to give vendors)
and 1099 data (CSV). To e-file, download the IRS template in IRIS and copy
the columns from the CSV into it.

INVENTORY
Edit (or create) an Inventory part and tick "Track quantity on hand". Enter
the quantity you have now and its value. Buy stock with a bill, check or
expense: pick the item, enter Qty and Cost - the line goes to Inventory
Asset. When you sell it, VaultBook records the cost of goods sold at the
average cost. Products and services -> Adjust inventory corrects counts.
Reports -> Inventory Valuation Summary and Inventory Stock Status.

FIXED ASSETS AND DEPRECIATION
Lists -> Fixed assets: add each asset (cost, date placed in service,
method: straight-line, double-declining or MACRS, and the accounts). Choose a
date and Post depreciation entry - VaultBook posts what is due and not yet
posted. Report: Fixed Asset Register.

DISCOUNTS, CREDITS AND REFUNDS
Early-payment discount: on a customer or vendor enter e.g. 2 % within 10
days. New invoices and bills carry it. On Receive Payment / Pay Bills, tick
"Take $x" next to the document while the discount is open; the payment and
the discount credit (Sales Discounts / Purchase Discounts) save together.
Apply credits: Receive Payment and Pay Bills list the contact's open credit
memos and unapplied payments under the documents; choose an amount and the
document to use each on, or tick "Use credits first".
Vendor refund: Vendors -> Vendor refund records money a vendor paid back
against a vendor credit. Refund deposit: a customer's page -> Refund deposit.

BOUNCED PAYMENTS
Open the customer payment -> More -> Record bounced payment. Enter the date
and any bank fee and customer fee. The payment stays as it was; a new charge
puts the amount back on the customer's balance and takes it out of the bank.

VOID AS OF A LATER DATE
A transaction in a closed or reconciled period can be voided "as of" a date
in an open period: the original stays in its period as reported and a
reversing entry is posted on that date.

ASSET DISPOSAL
Lists -> Fixed assets -> Dispose... on the asset: enter the date, the
proceeds and where they went. VaultBook posts depreciation to that date
(MACRS: half a year in the disposal year), clears the asset's cost and
accumulated depreciation and books the gain or loss. A warning shows when
the mid-quarter convention applies. "Tax depreciation" keeps a MACRS
figure on the register while the book method posts.

ATTACHMENTS
Open a saved transaction -> Attachments -> Attach file. Click a file name to
open it. Files are saved in this company and in its backups.

JOBS AND BILLABLE COSTS
On a customer's page choose + New job (or set "Job of" when editing a
customer). Use the job like a customer on invoices. On a bill, check or
expense line pick the Customer:Job and tick Billable; when you invoice that
customer choose + Billable costs to add them. Reports: Profit and Loss by
Job, Unbilled Costs by Job.

TAX LINES
Lists -> Tax lines: choose your income tax return, press Suggest from account
names, check the choices and save. Reports -> Income Tax Summary totals each
line; Tax export (CSV) is for your tax preparer.

SALES ORDERS AND PROGRESS INVOICING
A sales order (or estimate) does not affect your books. Open it and choose
Create Invoice: invoice everything left, or a percentage of each line
(for example 30% now, the rest later). It closes when it is fully invoiced.
A purchase order can be billed in parts the same way.

BATCH INVOICING
Sales -> Batch invoicing: enter the lines once, tick the customers and
create one invoice for each, with their own prices, terms and sales tax.

CUSTOMER DEPOSITS (RETAINERS)
On the customer's page choose Receive deposit. The money is held in
Customer Deposits until you invoice; on the invoice choose + Apply deposit.

PRICE LEVELS
Lists -> Price levels: create a level (e.g. Wholesale -20%, or a price per
item) and choose it on the customer. Picking an item on that customer's
invoice uses their price.

SALES TAX RETURNS AND PAYMENTS
If your rate combines several agencies (state + county + city), enter each
part in Settings -> Sales tax. Reports -> Sales Tax Return by Agency gives the
figures for each return. Banking -> Pay sales tax writes one check to each
agency. Record exemption certificates on tax-exempt customers and attach a
copy on the customer's page.

MORE REPORTS
A/R Collections (who to call), Missing Checks (gaps and duplicate numbers),
Voided/Changed Transactions, Cash Flow Forecast (next 12 weeks).

SALES TAX
Settings and backup -> Settings -> Sales tax: add each rate you charge (name,
rate %, agency, and the liability account it is owed in - usually Sales Tax
Payable). A combined rate (state + county + city) is entered as one rate.
Invoices, sales receipts, credit memos and estimates then show a Tax column
and a Sales tax choice. Tick Tax on the taxable lines (products marked
Taxable tick it for you). Tax = rate x taxable lines, rounded to the cent,
and is owed in the liability account - it is not income. Customers marked
Tax-exempt get "No sales tax"; a customer can also have their own rate.
Reports -> Sales Tax Liability shows taxable and non-taxable sales, tax
charged and what you owe. To pay the agency, write a check (or record an
expense) with the line account Sales Tax Payable.

CLOSING DATE PASSWORD
Settings -> Company: with a closing date password set, the closing date (and
so closed periods) cannot be changed without it. Every change of the closing
date is listed under Closing date history.

AUDIT TRAIL
Every change is recorded in the audit log, and each entry is sealed with a
SHA-256 hash of the entry before it. Settings -> Data integrity reports if an
entry was changed or removed.

QuickBooks Desktop (.iif): Transactions -> QuickBooks .iif lists your invoices
(filter by dates, customer, open only); tick the ones you want and download.
In QuickBooks Desktop: File -> Utilities -> Import -> IIF Files. Tick "Include
account, customer and item lists" if the QuickBooks file doesn't have them
yet. One invoice: open it -> QuickBooks .iif. Back up the QuickBooks company
file before importing.

If you save something that looks identical to an existing transaction (same
type, date, amount, name and account) you are warned before it is saved.


4. BANKING
----------
BANK FEED (Banking -> Bank feed)
The Bank feed is where bank and card transactions are reviewed. Each account
has a tile showing the bank balance from your last file and the balance in
VaultBook, plus how many lines wait for review.
1. Import file: choose the account and drop a CSV, Excel, OFX/QFX/QBO or PDF
   file. VaultBook picks the columns (date, description, amount or money
   in/out, check number, running balance) and the date format. Check them; if
   every date could be read as month/day or day/month you are warned. Tick
   "Remember these columns" and the next file from the same bank skips this
   step. A file that is not a bank export is refused with the reason.
2. The preview shows how many lines are new, how many were already imported
   (skipped), and whether the running balance checks out. Add them to For
   review. Nothing is posted on import.
3. For review: each line shows a match (an entry you already recorded, the
   other side of a transfer, or an open invoice or bill for that amount) or a
   category from your rules or from what you used last time for that
   merchant. Click Match or Add to post it, or click the line to open it:
   Categorize, Find match (all candidates with the reasons), Transfer, or
   Split (the difference must reach $0.00). Create rule and Exclude are there
   too.
4. Select several lines (or "Select them" on the ready banner) to post them
   together, set one category for all, or exclude them.
5. Posted: Undo returns a line to For review. A match is unlinked (your entry
   stays); a transaction the Bank feed created is voided.
6. Excluded lines keep their reason and can be restored. Imports lists every
   file; Undo import removes a file's lines while none of them is posted.
Keys in the list: Up/Down move, Enter open, Space select, A post, X exclude,
Esc close. Columns lets you show the check number, payee or bank balance and
rows per page; Comfortable/Compact sets the row height. Both are remembered.
Reconcile (top right) opens with the statement date and ending balance taken
from the last file, and lines posted from the Bank feed are already ticked.

MENU BAR AND TOOLBAR (1.51)
- The menu bar at the top holds every screen: File, Edit, View, Lists,
  Company, Customers, Vendors, Employees, Banking, Accountant, Reports,
  Window, Help. Window lists the screens you have open.
- Keyboard: Tab to the bar, Down opens a menu, Left and Right move between
  menus, Esc closes.
- The icon toolbar under it opens the most used screens.
- View > Show the side menu brings back the old side menu (Ctrl+\).
- File > Import, Export and Print are the old O, X and P menus.
- A company opens on Home. View > Gateway opens the keyboard menu.

THE SCREEN (1.25)
- Top bar: search (Ctrl+K), the current period (click or Alt+F2), + New
  (F10). Since 1.51 the K, Y, O, X and P menus are under File; their Alt
  keys still work.
- Home has two tabs. Get things done is the Gateway menu you drive with the
  keyboard (G H). Business overview (G D) shows your numbers: profit and
  loss for the last 30 days, expenses, bank accounts and how many lines
  wait for review, invoices, and a task list. Click any figure to open the
  transactions behind it.
- + New lists everything you can create. Type to filter, use the arrow keys
  and Enter, or press the F-key shown next to a form. Recently used entries
  open as a copy.
- The Banking menu shows a count when bank lines wait for review.

REGISTER GRAPHS AND OPTIONS (1.25.1)
- Sales and Purchase Registers show a bar per month under the table; click a
  bar to see that month's vouchers.
- Period, Show and Filters open with "Filters and options" (or Ctrl+F).

QUICKBOOKS ONLINE PATTERNS (1.26)
- Bookmarks: Ctrl+D or the star in the top bar adds the page you are on to
  the side menu; press it again to remove it. Edit (next to Bookmarks)
  renames and reorders them.
- The gear in the top bar (Ctrl+Alt+L) opens company settings, lists and
  tools, and turns the compact side menu (icons only) on or off.
- QuickBooks Online keys work too: Ctrl+Alt+I invoice, W check, X expense,
  E estimate, R receive payment, C customers, V vendors, A chart of
  accounts, F search, H help, Ctrl+Alt+/ all shortcuts. On a form,
  Ctrl+Alt+S saves and starts a new one, Ctrl+Alt+D saves and closes,
  Ctrl+Alt+P prints, Ctrl+Alt+X closes.
- Bank feed: open a line in For review and use "Date and note" to fix its
  date or leave a note before you post it.
- Reports: Chart shows bars above the report; Customize opens the options
  panel; More > Classic layout gives the older toolbar.
- Business overview: Customize chooses and orders the widgets; Privacy
  hides the amounts.
- A customer or vendor page has tabs for transactions, statements (or open
  bills), details and notes.
- On an invoice, "Preview and customize" shows the printed invoice beside
  the form.

ONE DEPOSIT, SEVERAL INVOICES (1.26.1)
- When a customer pays several invoices in one bank deposit, open the line
  in the bank feed. If the invoices add up, Find match offers them
  together; choose Match.
- To pick them yourself: Find match > Match open invoices yourself, choose
  the customer, tick the invoices, lower an amount to pay part of an
  invoice, then Match once the difference is $0.00.

ONE INVOICE, SEVERAL DEPOSITS (1.26.2)
- If a customer pays one invoice in two or more parts, open one of the
  bank lines: Find match shows "Part payment of Invoice ... with ..."
  when the other lines from that customer add up to the invoice. Match it,
  then match the next line, which now fits what is left.
- The customer's name has to be in the bank text of each line; amounts
  alone are never joined.

FIND: SEARCH ALL COMPANY DATA (1.27)
- Press Ctrl+F (or gear > Tools > Find). Type what you remember, e.g.
  "invoices over 5000 from acme may 2026", "1162", "$370,074.75",
  memo:"cash paid", "checks this year", "overdue".
- Every date is searched unless you name a period.
- Simple tab: type, name, period, number, amount.
- Advanced tab: add conditions, choose all or any, pick a period (All
  dates or your own From/To), then Find. Save search keeps it for next
  time; Export gives CSV or Excel.
- The tags under each memo say where the match was found.

RECONCILIATION REPORT (1.27.2)
- After Finish, or from Reconcile > history, the report opens. Detail
  lists every cleared and uncleared item with running balances; Summary
  shows the totals. Download PDF gives a print-ready file; Excel and CSV
  give the same rows.

PAYMENTS SENT TO QUICKBOOKS DESKTOP (1.27.3)
- Export to QuickBooks Desktop sends customer payments as Payments and
  bill payments as Bill Payments, with the invoices they pay in the memo,
  and downloads "payments to apply" (.csv) with the file.
- QuickBooks cannot link imported payments to invoices. In QuickBooks,
  open each payment (or Customers > Receive Payments) and tick the invoices
  from the list. With Edit > Preferences > Payments > Automatically apply
  payments on, QuickBooks applies each to an invoice of the same amount or
  else the oldest.
- If an earlier file left invoices open: export panel > Payments to apply
  for lines already exported, then in QuickBooks Receive Payments for the
  customer > Discounts and Credits on each invoice > tick the credit.

POST TO THE BOOKS AND DOWNLOAD .IIF (1.35)
- Payroll > Paystubs to journal: after the paystubs are read, the bar at
  the bottom has Entries (one per pay date, or one combined entry), "Post
  ... to the books" and "Download .iif for QuickBooks". Untick a pay date
  to leave it out of both.
- The .iif uses the accounts chosen in Payroll settings, with their
  VaultBook names. Tick "Create the accounts in QuickBooks first" if the
  client's QuickBooks file does not have them.

COMBINED PAYROLL ENTRY (1.34.1, standalone tool)
- Next to Download .iif choose Entries > "One combined entry for all
  ticked pay dates". Untick any pay date to leave it out. The .iif then
  holds one General Journal entry dated the last pay date.

PAYROLL DOCUMENTS AND CROSS-CHECK (1.34)
- Drop everything the client gave you for payroll into Payroll >
  Paystubs to journal (or the standalone tool): paystubs, payroll
  summaries/registers, EFTPS confirmations, Form 941, state withholding
  and unemployment returns and payments.
- "Cross-check of all payroll documents" lists each file, what it was
  recognised as and what it is used for, the employees and their states,
  a month-by-month table (wages, taxes, federal liability, deposit), and
  every check: green when the documents agree, red with the difference
  when they do not. "Not provided" lists what is missing.
- Only paystubs make journal entries; the other documents only check.

PAYSTUB TO QUICKBOOKS IIF (1.33.1, standalone tool)
- Open PaystubToIIF.html (works offline, any computer). Drop paystubs.
- Under "QuickBooks accounts" type each account name as it is in
  QuickBooks. Tick "Create these accounts in QuickBooks first" when the
  client's file does not have them yet.
- Download .iif, then in QuickBooks: File > Utilities > Import > IIF
  Files. Each pay date arrives as one General Journal entry.

CALENDAR: CLICK, DRAG AND UNDO (1.50.1)
- Click an empty spot of a day (or of an hour in Week and Day) to add a
  to-do. More options sets the time, repeat and reminder.
- Drag a to-do, bill, invoice or the next recurring entry to move it.
  Ctrl-click several to move them together. Undo, or press Ctrl+Z.
- In Week and Day, drag down in the grid to make a timed to-do, and drag
  the bottom edge of a to-do to change how long it is.

CALENDAR (1.50)
- Click Calendar in the sidebar. Month, Week or Agenda; tick the kinds
  of dates to show. Each day shows the forecast bank balance.
- Click a day to see its items. Pay a bill, set Pay on, or drag the
  bill to another day. Tick Filed / paid on a deadline.
- Settings: the low balance warning, payroll deadlines, reminders, and
  how often each sales tax agency is filed and on which day.
- Choose the income tax return in Tax lines so its deadlines show.
- Download for Outlook / Google saves an .ics file to open in your
  calendar.

DOC CENTER (1.49)
- Books > Doc Center. Drop receipts and bills on the page or click Add
  files. They wait in the Inbox.
- Each file shows the date, amount and vendor VaultBook read and the
  transaction it suggests. Correct a value and the suggestion follows.
  Click Attach, or Attach all sure matches.
- Missing documents lists spending of $75 or more with no file;
  download the list for the client, or attach a file on its row.
- Clean up removes copies, files on void transactions and large files.
- CPA package downloads a period's documents as one zip with an index.

SPLIT ONE AMOUNT OVER SEVERAL ACCOUNTS (1.48.1)
- In Excel, put the extra lines under the first row with the Date left
  blank; or give the rows the same Entry #; or list the accounts in one
  Split cell: Rent A 2000; Rent B 2000; Rent C (the last takes the
  rest) or Rent A 20%; Rent B 20%; Rent C 60%.
- In the grid, tick rows and click Join into one entry.

PASTE ENTRIES FROM EXCEL (1.48)
- Open a ledger and click Paste entries (or Books > Paste entries and
  choose the ledger).
- In Excel, select the rows (headings included is fine) and copy. Click
  a cell in the grid and press Ctrl+V.
- Debit and Credit are this ledger's side; Split is the other account.
- To repeat entries, tick the rows, enter the number of months and
  click Repeat monthly. Change dates, memos or amounts as needed.
- Accounts that are not in VaultBook are listed above the grid: choose
  what each becomes. Rows marked in red need a fix.
- Click Post entries. To take them back, click Void these.

EXCEL AND CSV BANK FILES (1.47.1)
- Bank feed > Import file takes .csv, .xlsx, .xlsm and .xls. VaultBook
  finds the header row and the sheet with the most transactions.
- Check the columns: Amount (minus = money out), or Money out and Money
  in. If the amounts have no minus signs and a Type or Dr/Cr column says
  Debit or Credit, choose it as the Debit / Credit column.
- Card accounts: Reverse signs is on when charges show as positive.
- Continue, then compare Money in and Money out with the statement.

UPDATE QUICKBOOKS TO MATCH VAULTBOOK (1.47)
- QuickBooks Desktop > Update QuickBooks. Back up the QuickBooks file.
- On the QuickBooks PC open the tool (Open the QuickBooks tool, or
  VaultBook QuickBooks.cmd > 7 Connect to VaultBook). First time only:
  run 8 Install the link so VaultBook can open it.
- Type the link code the tool shows and click Connect and get the
  export. VaultBook lists every edit and void.
- Click Send to the tool and answer Y/N in the tool's window. Then get
  a fresh export: the number of edits should be 0.
- Without the link: Download the update file and run 2 Send to
  QuickBooks with it; load a new 5 Export everything to check.

CLOSE THE BOOKS (1.46)
- Choose the year (or one month) at the top. Work down the eight steps;
  each shows Done or what is still open.
- Prepared by VaultBook: run the self-test first. It shows how many of
  last year's lines VaultBook would have posted and how many of those
  were right. Tick "Let VaultBook post the sure lines" and click
  Prepare. Lines it posted show their reason in the bank feed's Posted
  tab, where Undo puts them back.
- Review: pick the account once for a payee and Accept, or Ask client.
- Client questions: Copy or Download them for the client; type the
  answers back, then accept the payee in Review.
- Compare to last year: set the floor for this client; write a reason
  for each flagged account.
- Sign off is offered only when statements, review, questions,
  reconciliations and reasons are complete. It sets the closing date.

EXTRA HELP IN THE BANK FEED (1.45)
- In For review, choose Extra help: Other clients or Common merchants.
  The count shows how many lines with no suggestion it identified.
- Other clients uses the account names of your other companies on
  this computer. Companies with a password are not read.
- Each suggestion shows where it came from. Check it and click Add;
  these lines are not posted in bulk.

IMPORTING A GENERAL LEDGER FROM A PDF (1.44)
- Drop the General Ledger PDF together with the Balance Sheet and
  Profit and Loss PDFs for the same date. The two statements give
  the account types and are the check after the import.
- Print all three from QuickBooks on the same day, or the statements
  will show entries the ledger does not have.
- Long names and memos are cut where the printout cuts them. If the
  QuickBooks file is available, a General Ledger saved as CSV keeps
  the full text.
- A scanned ledger (a picture of paper) cannot be read.

IMPORTING A LEDGER WITH CLOSED YEARS (1.43)

The file (OfflineBooks_<Company>_<date>_<time>.json) contains everything for

  - Replace "<company>": overwrites that company's current data with the backup.

MOVING TO A NEW PC: back up on the old PC, copy the backup file and the app ZIP
to the new PC, extract, open index.html, Restore from backup.

12. COMPANY PASSWORD (SECURITY)
------------------------------
Each company can have its own password: Settings and backup -> Security.

WHAT IT DOES
- Everything stored for the company (accounts, customers, vendors, every
  transaction, rules, reconciliations, audit log) is ENCRYPTED on this PC with
  AES-256. Without the password the data can't be read, not even with the
  browser's developer tools or by copying the browser's files.
- Opening the company asks for the password. "Lock company" in the sidebar
  locks it immediately, and it locks itself after a period without activity
  (5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or never). When it locks, anything typed into an
  unsaved form is discarded.
- Backups you download are encrypted too (file name ends in _protected.json)
  and open only with the company password or recovery key. A protected backup
  restores as a protected company with the same password.
- Wrong passwords are rejected; after 3 wrong tries the unlock screen makes
  you wait a little longer each time.

TURNING IT ON
1. Settings and backup -> Security. Enter a password twice (at least 8
   characters; a short sentence is a good choice). Optional hint.
2. You are shown a RECOVERY KEY (32 characters, like XXXX-XXXX-...).
   Copy it, download it as a text file, or write it down, and keep it away
   from this PC. You must tick "I have saved the recovery key" to continue.
3. Download a new backup (it will be encrypted) and delete older backup files
   made before protection was on — those are NOT encrypted.

FORGOT THE PASSWORD
On the unlock screen: "Forgot password? Use recovery key". Enter the key (any
upper/lower case, dashes optional). You must then choose a new password, and a
NEW recovery key is issued — the used one stops working. Save the new one.
If both the password and the recovery key are lost, the data cannot be
recovered by anyone. This is what makes the protection real.

CHANGE / OFF
- Change password: Security -> Change password (current password required).
- Lost the recovery key but know the password: "Issue new recovery key".
- Turn off: decrypts the data back to normal storage (password required).
All of these are recorded in the audit log.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
- The company NAME is still shown in the company list.
- It does not protect against someone using the PC while the company is
  unlocked, or against malware such as keyloggers on this PC.
- It is one password per company, not separate user accounts or permissions.
- "Delete company" on the first screen still works without the password
  (deleting can't reveal data) — keep your backups.


13. COMMAND BAR, APPEARANCE AND ACCESSIBILITY
--------------------------------------------
COMMAND BAR (Ctrl+K, or "Search" at the top of the sidebar)
Type anything and press Enter:
- a transaction number ("INV-1004"), a customer or vendor name, a memo word,
  or an amount ("1,250.00") to open that transaction or contact;
- an account name or number to open its register;
- a report name ("aging", "balance sheet");
- an action ("new invoice", "pay bills", "reconcile", "download backup",
  "lock company").
With nothing typed it shows common actions and your most recent transactions.
Up/Down to move, Enter to open, Esc to close.

APPEARANCE
The sun/moon button at the top right switches instantly between Light and
Dark. Settings and backup -> App and storage also has "Match Windows", which
follows the Windows setting, and a Density choice: Comfortable (default) or
Compact (more rows on screen). Printing and Save as PDF always use the light
appearance. The sidebar can be hidden with the button at the top left (or
Ctrl+\) to give reports more room.
These preferences, and table column layouts, are saved in this browser and
kept when you close and reopen it. They are display settings only - not part
of your books or your backups.

ACCESSIBILITY
- Everything works from the keyboard: Tab moves through controls, every row in
  a list is a link (Tab to it, Enter to open), menus and suggestion lists use
  the arrow keys, Esc closes dialogs, panels and lists.
- Text meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1) in both light and dark appearance.
- Works with Windows Narrator and other screen readers (labelled controls,
  dialogs, list boxes and status messages).
- Respects the Windows "Show animations" setting (reduced motion).
- On narrow windows and phones, tables scroll inside themselves and
  transaction lines become stacked cards.


14. WORKING WITH LONG REPORTS AND TABLES
--------------------------------------
- Reports and lists fill the window. The title and filter bar stay in place
  and only the report or table scrolls; column headers stay at the top.
- Wide reports scroll sideways inside the report; the first (key) column -
  Date in the General Ledger, Account in the Trial Balance - stays pinned on
  the left.
- Resize a column: drag the right edge of its header. With the keyboard, Tab
  to the edge and press Left/Right (Shift for bigger steps). Double-click an
  edge to reset that column.
- Show or hide columns: "Columns" button (reports, Transactions, registers).
  The key column can't be hidden. "Reset layout" restores the defaults.
- Layouts are remembered per report or list. All account sections of the
  General Ledger share one layout so their columns line up.
  Settings -> App and storage -> "Reset all table layouts" clears them all.
- Reloading the page keeps you on the report or list you were viewing.

15. SAVING AND UNSAVED CHANGES
-----------------------------
- When you change a transaction or the company settings, the top bar shows
  "Unsaved changes" and the title shows "Edited". Undoing your edits clears it.
- Saving shows "Saving..." then "Saved". The Save button is disabled while
  saving, so double clicks or pressing Ctrl+S again can't post twice.
- If saving fails (for example the disk is full), you see "Save failed" with
  the reason and a "Try again" button. Nothing is posted and everything you
  typed stays on screen.
- If you try to leave with unsaved changes (a sidebar link, the Back button,
  Close, switching or locking the company), you are asked: Keep editing,
  Discard changes, or Save. Closing the browser tab also warns you.


16. COMPANY SNAPSHOT
-------------------
Sidebar -> Company Snapshot (or Ctrl+K and type "snapshot"). A one-page view
of your business made of panels. Every number is calculated from your posted
transactions - click any bar, slice, name or amount to open the detail behind
it (P&L for that month, the account's ledger, the invoice, the customer).

TABS
- Company:  Income and Expense Trend, Previous Year Income Comparison,
            Previous Year Expense Comparison, Account Balances, Customers Who
            Owe Money, Top Customers by Sales, Expense Breakdown.
            Also available: Vendors to Pay, Top Vendors by Expense, Income
            Breakdown, Key Figures, Reminders, Aging Summary.
- Payments: Aging Summary, Customers Who Owe Money, Invoice Payment Status,
            Recent Payments Received, Reminders, Vendors to Pay (+ Key Figures).
- Customer: choose a customer at the top; Customer Summary (balance, overdue,
            sales, average days to pay, last payment), Open Invoices, Customer
            Sales Trend (this year vs last), Recent Transactions.

ARRANGING PANELS (each tab keeps its own layout, per company)
- Move: drag a panel by its title bar and drop it anywhere - in any column, at
  any position. Keyboard: Tab to the panel's handle (six dots) and use the
  arrow keys (Up/Down within a column, Left/Right to the next column,
  Home/End for top/bottom). The new position is announced to screen readers.
- Columns: choose 2, 3 or 4 at the top. Panels keep their reading order.
- Remove: the X on a panel. It goes back into "Add content".
- Add content: opens a gallery with live previews of everything not shown.
  Click Add on each one you want, then Done. New panels go to the shortest
  column.
- Collapse: the ^ button shrinks a panel to its title bar.
- Expand: the diagonal-arrows button opens the panel full size with ALL of
  its data (every account, every open invoice), and has a Print button.
- Restore defaults: puts this tab back to its original panels, positions and
  settings. Other tabs are not touched.
Panel buttons appear when you point at a panel or Tab into it.

PANEL OPTIONS (the sliders button on each panel)
Each panel has its own settings, applied instantly and remembered:
- Date range: this fiscal year to date, this/last fiscal year, last 12
  months, this quarter, this/last month.
- Show as: Chart or Table (exact figures; also best for screen readers).
- Panel-specific choices, for example: group by month or quarter; compare
  yearly / quarterly / monthly and how many years; one account or all;
  which accounts to list (bank & cards, assets, liabilities, all, or pick
  them one by one); sort order; hide zero balances; each invoice or totals
  per customer; overdue only; top 5/10/20; how many slices in a breakdown;
  receivables or payables aging.
"Reset this panel" in the options returns that panel to its defaults.
